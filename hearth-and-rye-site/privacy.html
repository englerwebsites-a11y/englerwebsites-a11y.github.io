Privacy Policy

The short version: we only use your information for the reason you gave it to us, and we never sell it.

We don't sell your information

Hearth & Rye does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to other companies, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Creating an account

When you create an account, we ask for details like your name, email address, and phone number. This information is used only to set up and run your account: signing you in, showing your account details, and keeping track of your rewards.

The contact form

When you send us a message, we receive your name, email address, and message. We use this only to read your message and get back in touch with you.

Services that help run this site

Account information is stored securely with Firebase, a service run by Google, and contact form messages are delivered to us through a form service called FormSubmit. These services handle your information only so the site can work. Your browser also remembers things like your cart and login on your own device.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, send us a message through the contact form on the home page.

How it works

What your points mean

Hearth & Rye Rewards is our way of saying thanks for showing up — whether that's a daily coffee run or a Saturday morning with the whole family. Every purchase you make, in-store or through the site, adds points to your account automatically once you're signed in. There's no card to carry and nothing to remember at checkout; your points just build in the background until you're ready to use them.

Here's the math: for every dollar you spend at Hearth & Rye, you earn 5 points, and each point is worth one cent (1¢) when you redeem it. That works out to about a nickel back for every dollar spent — so a $20 order earns you 100 points, or roughly $1 in redeemable value. Points never expire as long as your account stays active, and they can be combined with seasonal specials and used on almost anything on the menu.

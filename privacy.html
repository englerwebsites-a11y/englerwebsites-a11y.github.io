How we handle your information

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 10, 2026

What we collect

When you reach out through our contact form, we collect whatever information you choose to share with us — typically your name, email address, phone number (if provided), business type, and any details about your project.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and discuss your website project with you. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other individuals, businesses, or third parties.

Discussing your project

Before we begin any work, we'll talk with you directly about what we can and can't do with any information, files, images, or other content you provide for your website, so there are no surprises about how your materials are used.

Once your website is live

Once we publish your website, anything you choose to include on it — text, images, contact details, or anything else — becomes public and visible to anyone who visits the site. This is separate from the private information you share with us during the inquiry process, which is never published without your knowledge.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact information only as long as it's needed to complete your project or respond to your inquiry. If you'd like us to delete your information at any point, just let us know and we will.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, email us at EnglerWebsites@gmail.com.

This policy covers information submitted through our contact form. It doesn't currently cover cookies or site analytics, since we don't use any at this time — we'll update this page if that changes.

FAQ

The questions we hear most — answered up front, no email required.

  • How much does a website cost?
    Website pricing depends on the number of pages, the level of design detail, and how much time the project takes. Rather than posting a generic quote, we prefer to walk through the details with you on an initial call — by the end, we'll agree on a set price together.
  • Do I have to pay anything up front?
    Nothing is due up front, and an initial consultation to talk through your website is completely free. Payment is due once the site is complete — after that, we publish it live and hand everything over to you.
  • You're a new business — why should I trust you with this?
    Being new doesn't mean we don't know what we're doing — take a look at our demo websites to see the quality of work we produce. We're also especially invested in doing right by early clients, since every project helps us build our reputation and prove what we can do.
  • Do I own the website once it's finished?
    Yes — once your site is complete, we can transfer full ownership to you, or continue managing it if you'd like ongoing maintenance and support.
  • Can I update the site myself after it launches?
    Yes. Once your site is complete, you'll have access to make your own updates and changes whenever you'd like.
  • How long does a build take?
    Build time depends on the size of the site and how many revisions you'd like along the way — we build at your pace and welcome your input throughout. As a general guide, a 5-page site takes us about a week. That gives us time to test everything carefully so your site launches without issues.
  • What if I don't like the design?
    We're happy to make revisions until you're satisfied with the result.
  • What if my business needs more than 5 pages?
    Absolutely — we can build sites larger than 5 pages. Larger sites are priced based on their scope and take a little longer to build.
  • How do I get started?
    Reach out whichever way is easiest for you. If you already know what you want, fill out our order form and we'll get back to you with a price. If you'd rather talk first, text us at (530) 691-2191 or email EnglerWebsites@gmail.com and we'll set up a time for a call. You can also send a message through the form on our Contact page. We typically reply within a day.

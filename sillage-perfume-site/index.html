How it works

One box, two ways to build it.

Every subscription is the same size and the same price — the only choice is how the bottle gets picked.

Vol. 01

Pick your path

On the collection page, choose "Surprise me" for a discovery pick, or "Choose my own" to select a specific fragrance yourself.

Vol. 02

We prepare your bottle

Chosen orders are poured to the fragrance you picked. Randomized orders are matched from the families you tend to reach for.

Vol. 03

It arrives monthly

A full-size bottle ships every month. Swap your pick, switch modes, or pause anytime from your account.

Choose your mode
